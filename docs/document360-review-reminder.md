# Document360 - How to Set Review Reminders

Review reminders in Document360 alert project members when an article needs to be reviewed or refreshed. Reminders can be set at an individual article level or applied to a group of articles in bulk.

---

## Single Article Review Reminder

To add a review reminder to an individual article:

1. Open the article in the Documentation editor.
2. Click the **More icon** in the article header and select **More article options**.
3. The **Article settings** pane will appear with the **Review reminder** tab selected by default.
4. Enter a descriptive reason for the review (provides context for the reviewer).
5. Choose a trigger date for the reminder:
   - **Now** — flags the article for review immediately.
   - **30 days** — triggers the reminder after 30 days.
   - **3 months** — triggers the reminder after 3 months.
   - **Custom date** — pick a specific date.
6. Click **Save**.

When an article needs review, a red badge (article status indicator) will appear to the left of the article title in the documentation editor, and the article header will show a **"Needs review"** tag.

---

## Bulk Article Review Reminders

To apply reminders to multiple articles simultaneously:

1. Navigate to **Content tools → Documentation → Article Review Reminders**.
2. Click **Create review reminder** (or **Add reminder**).
3. Enter a **Reminder name**.
4. Select the **repetition frequency** for the reminder.
5. Add the desired **reviewers** (reminder recipients).
6. Click **Next**.
7. Select the desired **Workspace** and **Language**.
8. In the **Select Articles** area:
   - Use **Apply filters** to narrow selection by author, category, date, or tags.
   - Select the articles you want to include.
9. Optionally check **"Set this rule for future articles/tags/contributors"** to automatically apply the same review reminder to matching articles in the future.
10. Click **Add** to create the reminder.

The system will notify selected team members via email when articles require review.

---

## Default Review Reminder (Project Settings)

To enable a default review reminder for all articles:

1. Go to **Settings → Knowledge base portal → General → Documentation**.
2. Under the **Review reminder** section, enable the toggle.
3. Set the desired number of days (1 to 365 days).
4. Turn on the **Default review reminder** toggle to enable automatic reminders.

This applies a review reminder to all articles in the project automatically.

---

## Clearing / Marking as Reviewed

Once an article has been reviewed and updated:

1. Navigate to the article.
2. Click the **"Needs review"** tag in the article header.
3. Click **Mark as reviewed**.
4. The "Needs review" tag is removed, and the article status reverts to its previous Draft/Published state.

---

## Bulk Review Operations (All Articles Page)

To manage review reminders in bulk:

1. Navigate to **Documentation** in the left navigation bar, then to **All articles**.
2. In the **Filter** option, expand the **Review reminder** dropdown and select **Stale**.
3. Select the articles you want to update.
4. To mark as reviewed: Click the **Mark as Reviewed** option above the list.
5. To set reminders: Click **Review reminder** and choose the intended reminder days.

---

## Tasks Integration

Review reminder tasks appear in the **Tasks** page alongside Workflow and Feedback tasks. Quick actions available for review reminder tasks include:

- **Publish**
- **Publish later**
- **Snooze**
- **Add labels**
- **Mark as reviewed**

---

## Visual Indicators

| Indicator | Meaning |
|-----------|---------|
| Red badge next to article title | Article needs review |
| "Needs review" tag in header | Article has been flagged for review |
| "Needs review" status | Takes precedence over all other article statuses |
